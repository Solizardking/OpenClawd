## Why are you interested in participating in the Fellows program?

I want to work on the practical safety problems that show up when frontier models stop being chat interfaces and become operators of software, money, browsers, APIs, and physical systems. My OpenClawd work has been a self-directed attempt to build that kind of system end to end: agent runtimes, MCP tools, browser extensions, payment-gated APIs, Solana wallet boundaries, robotics command envelopes, and public release hygiene. The recurring lesson is that the hard parts are not just model quality; they are permissioning, context integrity, tool isolation, observability, reproducibility, and knowing when an agent should not act.

Anthropic is the place I most want to learn from because the company treats those problems as core research and engineering questions rather than add-ons. I would bring a builder's perspective: I can take ambiguous research goals, turn them into working systems, find where the safety boundary breaks, and produce concrete demos, tests, and documentation. I am especially interested in contributing to work that makes Claude and Claude Code safer and more reliable when connected to real developer environments and external tools.

## Research areas I am excited about

I am excited about frontier-agent security: evals and mitigations for tool-using models that can browse, code, call MCP servers, operate wallets, and interact with privileged local state. Specific areas I want to work on include permission models for agent actions, context-injection resistance in coding and browser workflows, secure tool-use protocols, red-team harnesses for autonomous agents, and observability systems that make model/tool failures auditable. I am also interested in reinforcement-learning-style self-improvement loops where agents propose experiments and improve policies, but only inside bounded environments with clear reward definitions, risk limits, and rollback paths.
